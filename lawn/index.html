Lawn renovation log · 2026

From patchy to thick & green

A month of photos from the same three spots, lined up so each spot keeps the same angle. Drag across a photo to compare, or step through the dates.

How the photos were adjusted. Within each spot, every photo was matched to one reference shot using fixed landmarks (sidewalk joints, the mailbox post, the curb corner), then rotated, zoomed and cropped to that frame. Dark edges mean the original photo didn't reach that part of the frame. Nothing inside the photos was painted or retouched.

Day counts start at the first photo on Aug 21. Times are Eastern.

How to read the tags

  • Confirmed: times come from the place's own site, its Google business listing, or a post or review from the last 12 months or so.
  • Likely: a chain's standard schedule, a third-party guide, or evidence 1–3 years old, or times I had to partly infer (marked "times approximate").
  • Unverified: only old listings or thin evidence. Hidden unless you turn on "Include unverified".

Where this came from

I pulled every bar, pub, restaurant with a bar, chain, brewery and lounge Google Maps shows in the area (about 550 results, trimmed to about 190 real candidates), then did the same sweep for Taylor down to Eureka Rd (about 45 more). For each one I checked Google's happy-hour tags and business hours, searched its Google reviews for "happy hour" and noted how recent they were, then checked official sites, Facebook and Instagram posts, and happy hour guides.

Before you go

Bars change specials often, and chain happy hours vary by location. Times ending after midnight count toward the night they start. Several older listings (Double OO Pub, J R's Lounge, La Chambre, Bar Louie Dearborn, Hooters Taylor) turned out to be permanently closed, and Cavill's Lounge and Points n Pints in Taylor are temporarily closed. All were dropped.

How to read the tags

  • Confirmed: times come from the place's own site, its Google business listing, or a post or review from the last 12 months or so.
  • Likely: a chain's standard schedule, a third-party guide, or evidence 1–3 years old, or times I had to partly infer (marked "times approximate").
  • Unverified: only old listings or thin evidence. Hidden unless you turn on "Include unverified".
  • The dot next to "Why I think so" shows how fresh the evidence is: green within 6 months, amber 6–18 months, grey older or undated.

Where this came from

I pulled every bar, pub, restaurant with a bar, chain, brewery and lounge Google Maps shows across the west side (about 550 results, trimmed to about 190 real candidates), then did the same sweep for Taylor down to Eureka Rd (about 45 more). For each one I checked Google's happy-hour tags and business hours, searched its Google reviews for "happy hour" and noted how recent they were, then checked official sites, Facebook and Instagram posts, and happy hour guides.

      Tailgate intel

      What recent coverage says about the tailgate scene this season, plus every hour we re-checked for Sunday. Reddit blocks automated access, so none of this comes from Reddit threads. It's from venue sites, Eastern Market, Ford Field and local news.

      Eastern Market is ticket-only

      • Every space is sold online in advance through Passage. There are no walk-up sales.
      • Wristbands are color-coded for each game and can't be transferred. The ticket holder has to stay with the space.
      • Lots run 8 AM–5 PM Sunday. At the Sept 13 opener, crews were set up by 9 with beer pong, lawn games and full grills.
      • Big games have sold out ahead of time. Vivio's owner says playoff days doubled his usual business.
      • For you: hang on the edges at Vivio's, Bert's, EMBC and the Sunday Market (10–4, with a craft market in Shed 5). Don't wander into anyone's space.

      Free, no-ticket tailgate

      • Pride Plaza on Brush St, between the stadiums, starts at 10 AM. It has food trucks, Bud Light bars, a DJ and games, and runs until kickoff.
      • Ford Field's gates and Power Hour deals (10:30) are only for Lions ticket holders, so they don't apply to you.

      Want your own tailgate?

      • Gratiot 1000 (1000 Gratiot Ave, ~0.3 mi from Ford Field) is one of the few lots that allows it. It opens 8 AM and costs $70–300 a spot, with up to 5 people per car space, gas grills only, and tents needing an extra space.
      • Catch: it closes 2 hours after "the event." Confirm it covers the Tigers game before leaving a car there.
      • The garages next to Ford Field ban both tailgating and re-entry. They open about 4 hours before kickoff.
      • The $65 all-inclusive premium tailgate in Eastern Market's Shed 2 (9 AM–1 PM) is sold out.

      Tailgating west of Woodward

      • The lots around Little Caesars Arena and the District mostly ban it. 313 Presents says tailgating is prohibited in lots run by Olympia Development before, during and after events.
      • Park N Party lots west of Woodward allow it by their rules: 125 W. Elizabeth (35 spots, porta-potties, opens 3 hrs before, a block from Vollmer's), 1428 Griswold (20 spots, only around your car), 97 Winder by LCA, and 3420 Cass. All are gas grills only, and a tent needs an extra space.
      • Catch: when checked Saturday, none of those west-side lots showed a bookable date for Sunday. Only Gratiot 1000 (east side) did. Call Park N Party at 888-926-7275 before counting on one.
      • Closest thing to a west-side tailgate: Harry's parking-lot party (tent, bar, DJ or live music) runs for every home game, and its events page lists both of Sunday's games. Bookies' rooftop is the other open-air option.

      New since Friday

      • Plaza de los Leones: the Lions' game-day post (Sat, Sept 26) says the party starts at 10 AM on Brush St as Plaza de los Leones for Latino Heritage Month, with food trucks and entertainment. Last year's version had mariachi, ballet folklórico and a DJ. This year's lineup isn't posted.
      • Inside Ford Field, the first 25,000 fans get a Miller Lite trucker hat and there's a corgi race at halftime. That's for Lions ticket holders only.
      • Elwood still lists 9 AM–6 PM for Sunday. Detroit Beer Co. lists 10 AM–10 PM for the Lions game. Harry's lists both games and its lot party.
      • Detroit City Distillery's tailgate collab was for the Saints game. Nothing is posted for Sunday.
      • Bookies still shows "spring hours" (Sun 11–8) on its homepage, but its fall schedule says 10 AM on Lions days. Call 313-962-0319 to settle it.
      • Tin Roof's calendar page hasn't been updated in years, so its Sunday lineup (band on the roof 4–8 PM) may be stale.
      • Nothing new posted for Vivio's, Bert's, EMBC, Union Assembly, Brass Rail, Frita, Nemo's, Firebird, Old Shillelagh, Oktoberfest or Vollmer's, which still lists Sunday at 5 PM.

      Logistics

      Parking, gates, what to carry, and how to get out.

      Parking (book it Saturday)

      • Coming in separate cars? Book the same garage. Everyone starts and ends at the same spot, and the meetup is easy.
      • Pre-book on SpotHero or ParkWhiz. Lions days push close-in lots to roughly $40–80+. Lots a few blocks out run much cheaper.
      • Best base: Greektown or east of Ford Field. It's central to all three plays and about 10 minutes from Comerica for the walk back. The Hollywood Casino Greektown self-park garage is at 1211 Chrysler Dr; its game-day rate isn't posted, so check the apps.
      • Harry's Detroit (2482 Clifford) gives customers free parking plus a shuttle to Comerica and Ford Field. Confirm at 313-964-1575; the lot fills.
      • Stadium garages next to Ford Field ban tailgating and re-entry. For a tailgate with your car, Gratiot 1000 allows it (see Intel).
      • Eastern Market lots aren't general parking on Lions days. They're tailgate spaces sold ahead of time on easternmarket.org.
      • Street meters: Detroit enforces some zones on Sundays. Pay in the ParkDetroit app and read the signs.

      Comerica gates & rules

      • Gates open about 1 hr 40 min before first pitch, so around 1:30 PM.
      • No re-entry. Once you're in, you're in.
      • Bags: nothing bigger than a 4" × 6" × 1.5" pouch or wallet. No backpacks or purses. Leave them in the cars.
      • One factory-sealed water, 20 oz or smaller, plus one empty plastic bottle. No outside food.
      • Cashless. Cash-to-card kiosks are inside.
      • It's the last game under the Comerica Park name. It becomes Fifth Third Park in 2027, so get the photo out front.

      Carry list

      • Tickets loaded in the MLB Ballpark app on at least two phones
      • A pocket-size battery pack (it has to fit the tiny-bag rule)
      • ID, a card, sunglasses, sunscreen
      • A light layer for the 50s morning that you can tie around your waist by noon
      • A sober driver for each car, or a plan to rideshare home and grab the cars later

      Getting out

      • The Lions crowd clears out around 4:15 while you're still in the ballpark. By the final out (~6 PM), most of that traffic is gone.
      • Highway options are I-75, I-375/Gratiot heading east, and the Lodge (M-10). Check live traffic before you pull out.
      • Car closures near Greektown (Downtown Detroit Partnership): Brush St is closed to cars between Lafayette and Macomb through December. Monroe was listed closed to cars between Randolph and St. Antoine. Westbound Broadway is closed between Gratiot and Grand River. On foot, none of it matters. Driving in, come from the I-375 service drive (Chrysler Dr).
      • Free backup rides: the People Mover is free and runs Sundays 10 AM–10 PM, with stops at Greektown, Broadway, Grand Circus Park and Cadillac Center. The QLine streetcar on Woodward is free too.

      Closed Sundays (don't get burned)

      • Louie's Ham & Corned Beef, Eastern Market (Mon–Sat, 6 AM–4 PM)
      • Supino Pizzeria, Eastern Market (Tue–Sat)
      • Zeff's Coney Island, Eastern Market (Mon–Sat)
      • Cutter's Bar & Grill (listed Mon–Sat; call if you're set on the burger)
      • Hockeytown Cafe (only opens for events at Little Caesars Arena)
      • Cheli's Chili Bar has closed. Tin Roof is in that building now.

      Coneys?

      • Lafayette and American Coney Island sit on Lafayette Blvd by Campus Martius, which is outside the range you set.
      • Inside the range, your best bet for a hot dog is the ballpark's concession stands.

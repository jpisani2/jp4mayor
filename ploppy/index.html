How each spin is scored

Black pays 1 to 1 and the third column pays 2 to 1. At the starting bet, a big hit (6, 15, 24 or 33, both bets win) is +$7. A small hit (any other black number, or a red number in the third column) is +$1. A no-hit (a red number outside the column, or zero) is −$5.

A no-hit moves you up one level. A big hit sends you back to level 1. A small hit moves you down one level once your bankroll is back above where it was before the loss that moved you up. A session ends after the set number of spins, when you're ahead by your quit amount, or when you can't cover the next bet.

For fun and learning. Every result here comes from random simulated spins. No progression changes the house edge, so over time every system on this page loses money on average. Please gamble only what you can afford to lose.

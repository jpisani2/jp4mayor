About the history. The record is ERA5 reanalysis for the roughly 25 km grid cell around this spot, back to 1940. It tracks official station records closely but won't match them exactly. "Normal" means the 1991–2020 average, smoothed over a 15-day window. The record downloads once per location and is then kept in this browser.

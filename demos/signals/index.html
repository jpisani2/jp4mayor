How it works

1. When the coach signals, tap each sign in order.

2. After the snap, tap what happened. The signal gets saved with the result and clears for the next play.

3. The app tries different ways a coach might hide the real sign: the whole sequence, the 1st/2nd/last sign, the sign right after an "indicator" (like a hat touch), or whether one particular sign shows up at all. It checks each one against your past plays and uses whichever has actually been right most often.

4. Once a pattern repeats enough, entering the signal shows the call. Look in Insights to see which system seems to have cracked their code.

Tip: to see how it works, tap "Load sample game," then go back to Live and enter Nose › Hat › Chest.

This is the demo, so nothing is saved. Reloading brings the sample game back. (The real app saves everything on your phone.)
